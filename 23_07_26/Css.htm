PM Assures Students In First Message On Paper Leaks, Rahul Gandhi Hits Back

PM Assures Students In First Message On Paper Leaks, Rahul Gandhi Hits Back | New Delhi:

Amid raging student protests in the Capital and a showdown in Parliament, Prime Minister Narendra Modi on Thursday morning sent out a big message to the youth of the country.

"Nothing is more important than the welfare and future of our youth!" the Prime Minister posted on X, adding that the government has decided to set up fast-track courts to try paper leak cases.
